# Product Detail Page — Feature & Copy Reference

Adapted from a QNED TV product page's structure and language, rewritten for Consistent For Life's tone and brand rules (no source-brand naming, warm/confident/human copy).

## Page pattern to reuse

Feature name (small badge) → punchy H2/H3 headline → 1–2 sentence benefit copy → hero image or video. Group related features under named umbrella sections instead of a flat list. Sprinkle trust badges next to the specific feature they support rather than one lump "awards" section.

## Section flow (top to bottom)

1. Hero: product title, size/variant selector, star rating, "Talk to us" CTA
2. Image gallery: front views, dimension diagram, feature glamour shots
3. Key Features — 2-line elevator pitch
4. In-page nav: Features / Specs / Reviews / FAQ / Support / Where to Buy
5. "Why It's Built Different" — 4-icon summary strip
6. Deep-dive feature blocks (see below)
7. Specs table
8. Reviews, FAQ, Support, Where to Buy

## Features to include, with sample wording

**Picture quality tech** — "Rich, accurate color, certified" — short line on wide color gamut + a real third-party certification badge if we have one (or a placeholder "Color Accuracy Certified" badge for now).

**Processor / upscaling** — "Every frame, sharpened in real time" — one line on how the processor upscales lower-res content and balances brightness/contrast automatically.

**Sound** — "Room-filling sound, no extra speakers" — virtual surround sound framed as convenience, not spec-sheet jargon.

**AI Hub / smart assistant** — "Ask it, and it finds it" — multi-source search framed around ease, not naming specific AI vendors.

**Personal profile / home screen** — "Everything at a glance — weather, schedule, scores, all yours" — a personalized home screen feature.

**Proactive troubleshooting** — "It notices before you do" — an on-screen assistant that flags issues and walks the user through a fix.

**Picture/sound personalization wizard** — "Tell it what you like, it remembers" — guided picture/sound calibration by preference, not manual menus.

**Security/reliability badge** — "Consistent for life starts with what's under the hood" — a trust section on data security and build quality, tying directly into the brand theme.

**Free software upgrades over time** — "We keep it current, for years" — longevity promise, big brand-fit opportunity.

**Durability protections** — "Built to handle real life" — surge/humidity/heat protection framed around durability, not spec jargon.

**Remote/navigation** — "Point, scroll, done" — simple air-mouse-style remote interaction.

**Live sports/alerts** — "Catch every moment, even if you looked away" — game alerts and scores.

**Gaming mode** — "Built for winning, not just playing" — smooth refresh rate, low latency, one-tap game settings.

**Cinema/filmmaker mode** — "See it exactly as it was made to be seen" — a picture mode that preserves the original creative intent.

**Design/build** — "A finish that belongs in your home, not just your wishlist" — industrial design section, ties to "breathing space" photography principle.

## Image ideas per section

- **Hero / gallery:** clean studio product shot on neutral background, 2–3 angles, plus a simple dimension/spec diagram line drawing.
- **Color tech:** full-bleed abstract close-up of vivid on-screen color/motion — no product visible, just screen content filling the frame.
- **Processor/upscaling:** before/after split-screen using a real lifestyle scene (e.g., a person walking through a colorful outdoor setting) to show clarity improvement.
- **Sound:** modern living room, wide shot, someone relaxed on a couch with immersive content on screen (concert, film) — warmth + breathing space.
- **AI Hub / search:** UI screenshot/mockup of the home screen with personalized widgets (weather, schedule, recommendations).
- **Proactive assistant:** UI screenshot of an on-screen help prompt/chat bubble over a paused scene.
- **Picture/sound wizard:** grid of picture or sound preset thumbnails, UI-style, showing selection states.
- **Security/durability:** macro/detail shot of internal component or clean icon-based graphic (lightning, humidity, surge icons) on brand gradient background — no literal product photography needed here.
- **Free upgrades:** simple badge/icon graphic, brand gradient background, clock or upward-arrow motif suggesting longevity.
- **Remote/navigation:** product detail shot of remote in hand, candid, warm lighting — "Everyday Life" role.
- **Sports alerts:** lifestyle shot, group of friends/family watching a live match, genuine reaction, candid framing.
- **Gaming:** "Energy/Passion" role photo — person engaged in gameplay, controller in hand, screen glow, authentic excitement.
- **Cinema mode:** dim, cozy home theater/living room setup, one or two people watching, warm ambient lighting.
- **Design/build:** styled room shot, TV mounted or on a console, matched to real furniture and decor — shows the product belongs in a home, not a showroom. Include one macro shot of the back panel or edge detailing to sell finish quality.

## Notes

- Never name the source brand or its AI partners (Gemini, Copilot, etc.) — genericize as "AI Hub," "smart search," "voice assistant."
- Certifications/badges: only use real ones we can back up, or design a neutral "Consistent For Life Certified" style badge as placeholder.
- Keep the repeating rhythm (badge → headline → 1–2 lines → image) consistent across every feature block for scannability.
- "Protected for the long run" / "Free upgrades, for years" sections are the strongest natural fits for the Consistent For Life theme — consider expanding these beyond the source page's treatment.
